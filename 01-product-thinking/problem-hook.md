# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic Velocity
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Within 12 months RouteLogic Velocity likely loses its largest enterprise account at renewal, as the frontline teams who’ve already disengaged push leadership toward the leaner competitor they’re trialing. That loss signals to the rest of the market that “powerful” now means “slow,” which accelerates churn across mid-size fleets and leaves RouteLogic defending a shrinking niche of compliance-heavy power users.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** A dispatcher learns a driver is stuck and three deliveries will miss their window. Instead of clicking through RouteLogic’s layered menus to reassign stops, they call the driver, check Google Maps, and update a shared spreadsheet/WhatsApp/teams group so the team knows. RouteLogic becomes the system they update afterwards for compliance, not the tool they use to make the decision.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** RouteLogic has become too heavy for the people who run fleets in real time, and our biggest customer is already trialing the alternative.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** RouteLogic Velocity gives dispatchers and fleet coordinators a fast, focused workspace for the jobs they do every hour (track, reassign, communicate) with zero enterprise clutter in the way. Advanced capabilities like compliance and financial reporting stay fully available, but move into dedicated spaces for the people who need them in the right moments.

Why right now: Our largest account is evaluating a leaner competitor (so they are already in a decision tree), frontline usage is falling, and every month we wait makes “complex” a harder label to shake. Velocity lets us prove we can be both the most powerful and the fastest tool on the market.
